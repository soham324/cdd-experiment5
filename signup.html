SMART AGRICULTURE

Introduction to Smart Farming

Smart farming uses modern technology to improve agricultural productivity. It uses sensors, drones and machines to help farmers grow crops efficiently and save water.

Types of Crops

1. Food Crops

Rice, wheat, maize and millets are examples of food crops.

2. Cash Crops

Cotton, sugarcane, coffee and tea are grown for sale.

3. Horticultural Crops

Fruits, vegetables and flowers are horticultural crops.

Farming Methods

Traditional Farming

Uses traditional tools and methods to grow crops.

Organic Farming

Uses natural fertilizers and biological pest control.

Modern Farming

Uses machines and technology to increase productivity.

Modern Agriculture Technologies

  • Smart sensors to measure soil moisture.
  • Drones to monitor crops.
  • GPS to guide agricultural machines.
  • Artificial intelligence to detect crop diseases.
  • Automatic irrigation systems to save water

Farmer Tips

  • Use water carefully.
  • Test the soil before adding fertilizers.
  • Choose crops suitable for the climate.
  • Check crops regularly for pests and diseases.
  • Use good-quality seeds.
  • Check weather forecasts before farming activities.
